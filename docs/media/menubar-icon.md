# Lowfi menu-bar icon

`menubar.svg` is an original, monochrome “late-night note” drawn for Lowfi. Its softly irregular
note head, leaning stem, and curved flag take their visual direction from the flowing, modestly
asymmetric character of the existing [Lowfi logo](./logo.svg). It is released under this
repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

## Research and provenance

[Awesome Icons](https://github.com/notlmn/awesome-icons) was the primary discovery index. Its
entries for [Feather](https://github.com/feathericons/feather) and
[Tabler Icons](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons) helped evaluate rounded forms and compact
musical silhouettes at menu-bar sizes. Both projects are MIT licensed: [Feather's
license](https://github.com/feathericons/feather/blob/main/LICENSE) and [Tabler's
license](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/LICENSE) require their copyright and
license notices to remain when artwork is copied.

The supplied [awesome-pixel-art](https://github.com/Siilwyn/awesome-pixel-art),
[awesome-stock-resources](https://github.com/neutraltone/awesome-stock-resources),
[awesome-fonts](https://github.com/brabadu/awesome-fonts), and
[codeface](https://github.com/chrissimpkins/codeface) collections were secondary research only.
No artwork, font glyph, outline, or other asset from any reference was copied or redistributed.
Every path in `menubar.svg` was drawn independently, so no third-party attribution is required
for the bundled icon. Future use of copied Feather or Tabler artwork must retain the applicable
MIT notice and license.
